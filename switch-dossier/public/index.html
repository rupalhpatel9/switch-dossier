How the agents work

1 · Resolve & planMaps your drug name to FDA identifiers, finds alternatives, picks the biologic or small-molecule path.
2 · Retrieve in parallelFDA, patents & exclusivity, PubMed & ClinicalTrials.gov, NADAC pricing, state rules.
3 · Extract & verifySchema-enforced extraction. Each claim is checked against source text; unsupported claims are dropped.
4 · SynthesizeDossier with recommendation and stated limits. Conflicting studies are shown, not averaged.
For professional use by pharmacists and market access analysts. This is decision support, not patient-facing clinical advice. Expert review is required before any formulary action. All tools are read-only.
